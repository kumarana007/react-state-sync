# Contributing to react-state-sync

Thank you for your interest in contributing to `react-state-sync`! As an open-source project, we welcome community feedback, bug reports, and pull requests.

## Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for all contributors. Please treat all members of the community with respect.

## How Can I Contribute?

### 1. Reporting Bugs
- Check the **Issues** tab to see if the bug has already been reported.
- If not, open a new issue with a clear title and description.
- Include minimal reproducible code, your browser version, and your React version.

### 2. Suggesting Enhancements
- Open an issue describing the feature, why it is needed, and how it improves developer experience.
- Explain potential trade-offs or alternative solutions considered.
